About

Made by Julian Toquica.

I'm a 3D artist and designer specializing in the intersection of traditional art and generative AI. Modeling, texturing, animation, and AI-driven production — applied across film, games, branding, and interactive experiences.

I've worked as a 3D modeler on productions like El Violinista (winner at the 2026 Annecy Festival) and 40 Días Perdidos en la Selva, and on games like FIFA Rivals. Today I lead 3D art teams, pairing Blender, ZBrush, and Substance Painter with Midjourney and Weavy to push visual production further.

What drives me is technical curiosity — finding the visual solution that once seemed impossible. Sketch Render Studio was born from that same curiosity.
